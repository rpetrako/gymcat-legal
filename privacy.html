Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Gym Cat is made by Ranko Petrakovic, an individual developer in Zagreb, Croatia. It is an app for personal trainers: you record your clients (the app calls them trainees), their measurements, goals, plans, workouts, training periods and payments. This policy explains where that information is kept and who can see it.

The short version

The developer collects nothing. There is no server, no user account, no analytics and no advertising, and the developer cannot see anything you or your trainees enter.

What the app stores and where

  • On your iPhone: everything you enter about your trainees: names, addresses, photos you choose, notes, body measurements, goals, plans, workouts, training periods and payment records.
  • In your iCloud: if Sync with iCloud is on (Settings › iCloud in the app), the same information is backed up to your own private iCloud database, so it comes back on a new iPhone signed in to your Apple Account. This storage belongs to your Apple Account; Apple holds it under Apple's Privacy Policy, and the developer has no access to it.
  • Logs a trainee shares with you: a trainee who uses Gym Rat can share their workout log with you through iCloud. Their workouts, plans and rest times then stay in their own iCloud and are shared with you until they stop sharing or you remove them from Gym Cat; workouts you log for them and plans you send them are saved in that shared log. Everything else you record (address, notes, photos, measurements, goals, training periods and payments) is never shared with the trainee and stays with you.

Payments

Gym Cat records payments as information only: what you issued and whether it was paid. Money is moved outside the app. Gym Cat never asks for or stores bank or card details.

Reminders

Payment reminders, if you turn them on, are scheduled on your iPhone. Each one shows the trainee's name, the amount, the payment's note and whether it is paid. Where notifications are allowed on the Lock Screen, anyone who sees your iPhone can read them there; iPhone Settings › Notifications › Show Previews lets you hide them until the iPhone is unlocked. Nothing about them is sent to the developer.

Export and import

Export All (Settings › Data in the app) and Export <name>'s Data (on a trainee's profile) write CSV files and open the share sheet. The files leave the app only through the share sheet, to wherever you send them: Mail, Messages, AirDrop or a folder in Files. From then on they are wherever you put them, and Gym Cat can't change or delete them. The copies the app writes for the share sheet are deleted when it closes. Import reads CSV files you choose in Files and sends nothing anywhere.

Tracking and third parties

Gym Cat contains no third-party code for analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Your trainees' data and the GDPR

If you coach trainees as part of your work, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) treats you as the controller of what you record about them: you decide what is kept and why. In practice:

  • Tell your trainees what you record and keep only what you need.
  • Body measurements can count as data concerning health, which the GDPR protects more strictly. Ask your trainees for their consent before you record them.
  • When a trainee asks for their data, Export <name>'s Data on their profile writes CSV files you can give them: their name, address, notes, the day you added them, whether they are active and whether they share a Gym Rat log, and their training periods, measurements, goals, payments, plans and workouts. Their photo isn't in the export.
  • When a trainee asks you to delete their data, Delete Trainee removes them and their records from the app and, with Sync on, from your iCloud backup. Files you exported and sent earlier are wherever you sent them; delete those yourself.

The developer has no access to this data and is not a processor of it.

Deleting data

You can delete trainees and any of their records in the app. Deleting the app removes the data on that iPhone. Data in your iCloud can be removed in iPhone Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. If you record a trainee who is a child, the consent of a parent or guardian may be needed where you work.
